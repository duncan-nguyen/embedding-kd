\subsection{Protocol}
\label{app:protocol}

Every method is scored the same way, on frozen student embeddings with CLS
pooling and no fine-tuning of the encoder. Classification benchmarks fit a
logistic-regression probe on the benchmark's own training split and score the
test split; the probe train and validation files are checked for
normalized-text leakage against the test split before fitting. Pair benchmarks
map the cosine similarity of the two sentences to $[0,1]$; we report average
precision, which ranks pairs and therefore needs no decision threshold. STS
benchmarks correlate the raw cosine similarity with the gold score
(Spearman). The Avg.\ column of Table~\ref{tab:main-results} is the unweighted
mean of the nine primary scores.

The reported runs evaluate the test split after every epoch with no separate
validation pass, and the reported numbers are those of the final (fifth) epoch;
no checkpoint is selected on the basis of a test score. Threshold-dependent
accuracy and F1 for the pair family are produced by the same harness with the
threshold swept on the evaluation split, and are therefore not reported here.
