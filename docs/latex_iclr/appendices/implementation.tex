\subsection{Cost}
\label{app:cost}

The teacher is run once over the corpus and its normalized outputs are stored as
a single $102{,}361\times d_T$ float32 tensor (0.4\,GB at $d_T=1024$, 1.0\,GB at
$d_T=2560$). Fitting $P_{\mathrm{PCA}}$ is one truncated SVD of the cache;
fitting $R^\star$ is one $d_S\times d_S$ SVD over 16{,}384 sentences. Both are
computed before the first optimizer step and never updated, so training itself
sees only a fixed lookup table of targets and never loads the teacher.

\subsection{Hyperparameter matching across methods}
\label{app:hp-matching}

The baseline rows follow the recipe published with each method rather than a
single shared optimizer setting: TALAS keeps its SAM optimizer, and the
cross-tokenizer baselines keep the learning rate and batch size of their
reference implementations. The comparison in Table~\ref{tab:main-results} is
therefore between methods as they are normally run, not between objectives at
matched optimization settings, and part of the reported margin may be
attributable to that difference.
