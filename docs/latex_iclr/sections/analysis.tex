\subsection{Is the Bottleneck Compression or Comparability?}
\label{sec:analysis-interface}

Table~\ref{tab:interface-families} controls the endpoint objective while changing
only how teacher and student coordinates are made comparable. A random subspace
retains far less teacher energy than PCA yet gives a comparable downstream score.
For either fixed subspace, epoch-wise Procrustes improves the result without
changing the subspace itself. This suggests that endpoint comparability, rather
than dimensionality reduction alone, is the operative bottleneck.

\subsection{Which Signals Require an Interface?}
\label{sec:analysis-decomposition}

Table~\ref{tab:signal-decomposition} separates the coordinate-dependent endpoint
term from the coordinate-invariant connectivity term. Endpoint supervision
provides most of the downstream improvement, while native-space $H_0$ supervision
substantially reduces the persistent-connectivity residual. Their combination is
strongest when $H_0$ bypasses the endpoint projection and is computed from the
original teacher representation.
