\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:exp-setup}

\paragraph{Teacher--student pairs.}
We evaluate three teacher--student configurations that differ in teacher
family, teacher width, and student capacity:
(a) Qwen3-Embedding-4B ($d_T=2560$, last-token pooling) $\rightarrow$
BERT-base (109M, $d_S=768$, 12 layers), the capacity-gap setting;
(b) BGE-M3 ($d_T=1024$, an XLM-R encoder with CLS pooling) $\rightarrow$
MiniLMv2-L6-H768 (66M, $d_S=768$, 6 layers), a different teacher family;
(c) Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B ($d_T=1024$, last-token pooling) $\rightarrow$
MiniLMv2-L6-H384 (22M, $d_S=384$, 6 layers), the compact-student setting.
In every pair $d_T>d_S$, so the fixed map $P_T=P_{\mathrm{PCA}}R^\star$ of
Eq.~\ref{eq:projected-teacher} is required.

\paragraph{Training corpus.}
All methods are distilled on the same corpus of \textbf{102{,}361 unlabeled
sentences}, sampled once from the training splits of the nine evaluation
benchmarks (SciTail 23{,}795; Tweet 23{,}726; Emotion 15{,}956; STS-B 9{,}196;
Banking77 9{,}003; MRPC 7{,}052; WiC 6{,}333; SICK 4{,}802; STS12 2{,}498) and
fixed across every run in Table~\ref{tab:main-results}. Only raw text is read:
no label, pair annotation, or similarity score enters any objective, so the
training signal is unsupervised apart from the teacher. The teacher is run once
over the corpus before training and its $102{,}361\times d_T$ normalized output
embeddings are cached; no teacher hidden state, attention map, or forward pass
is used during optimization.

\paragraph{Optimization.}
Students are trained for 5 epochs with AdamW under mixed precision, a cosine
schedule with $6\%$ warmup decaying to $2\times10^{-6}$, a maximum sequence
length of 256 tokens, and seed 42. Pooling is CLS at every depth, which matches
the pooling used by the evaluation code, so the supervised endpoint is exactly
the embedding that is scored. Per-pair learning rates and batch sizes are given
in Appendix~\ref{app:implementation}.

\paragraph{Reported configuration.}
The rows labeled GeoODE-KD in Table~\ref{tab:main-results} use the endpoint and
contrastive terms of Eq.~\ref{eq:total-training-objective} with
$\lambda_{\mathrm{end}}=1$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{ctr}}=0.5$, and
$\lambda_{\mathrm{evel}}=0$; the contrastive temperature is $\tau_c=0.05$ and
the second view is an independent dropout mask. The target map is fitted once
before training --- PCA to $d_S$ on the cache, then the Procrustes rotation
$R^\star$ of Eq.~\ref{eq:gauge-alignment} estimated on 16{,}384 corpus
sentences against the untrained student --- and both factors are frozen for the
whole run. $\lambda_{\mathrm{ctr}}$ is therefore the only tuned weight, and no
parameter is added to the student at any point.

\paragraph{Baselines.}
We compare against Stella-style multi-dimension output distillation
\citep{zhang2024jasper}, EMO's optimal-transport embedding objective
\citep{truong2025emo}, TALAS multi-layer teacher anchoring \citep{talas}, and
two cross-tokenizer transfer methods, CDM (contextual dynamic mapping) and DSKD
(dual-space knowledge distillation).
% TODO: add bib entries for CDM and DSKD.
Each baseline follows its own published recipe, including TALAS's SAM
optimizer, which we leave in place rather than substituting AdamW. The
\emph{Teacher} row scores the teacher embeddings directly and the \emph{Student
base} row scores the student checkpoint before distillation; both bound the
range a distillation method can move within.

\paragraph{Evaluation.}
We score frozen student embeddings on nine benchmarks in three families. For
classification (Banking77, Emotion, Tweet) a logistic-regression probe is fitted
on that benchmark's own training split and scored with macro-F1. For pair
classification (MRPC, SciTail, WiC) the cosine similarity is mapped to $[0,1]$
and scored with average precision. For STS (SICK, STS12, STS-B) we report
Spearman correlation against gold scores. The Avg.\ column is the unweighted
mean of the nine primary scores. Appendix~\ref{app:evaluation} gives the split
sizes and the threshold protocol.

\subsection{Main Results}
\label{sec:main-results}

Table~\ref{tab:main-results} reports the three configurations. On both MiniLM
students the method is the strongest of the six on the average and on eight of
nine individual benchmarks (all but WiC): it reaches \textbf{78.29} against
76.14 for TALAS on pair (b) ($+2.15$) and \textbf{77.14} against 74.14 on pair (c) ($+3.00$), while
the best non-anchoring baseline stays near 71.9 and 69.2 respectively. On pair
(a), the capacity-gap setting, it reaches 79.69 and places second behind TALAS
(80.46, $-0.77$) while remaining $2.34$ points above the strongest remaining
baseline. Relative to the teacher, the distilled students recover $98.1\%$,
$98.2\%$, and $97.3\%$ of the teacher average on (a), (b), and (c), starting
from student checkpoints at $54.36$, $56.35$, and $52.60$.

The per-task pattern is consistent across pairs. The gains concentrate on STS
and on the classification probes --- on pair (c), $+5.60$ SICK, $+2.53$ STS12,
and $+2.77$ STS-B over TALAS, with $+4.77$ Banking77 and $+6.66$ Emotion --- and
the one family where the method does not lead is WiC, where the
projection-based baselines score $68.5$--$69.8$ against $63.5$--$67.0$ here.
The same trade-off appears in pair (a), where the residual gap to TALAS comes
almost entirely from STS12 ($-1.76$) and STS-B ($-2.30$); on SICK and Tweet the
ordering reverses.

\paragraph{Overlap between the corpus and two test splits.}
Because the corpus is sampled from benchmark training splits, some test
sentences recur in it as raw text: measured by exact match, $74.5\%$ of SICK
test sentences and $21.2\%$ of STS-B test sentences appear in the corpus, while
the remaining seven splits are at or below $9\%$ and STS12 is clean. No labels
are involved, but the two affected scores partly measure teacher agreement on
memorized text. Dropping SICK and STS-B leaves the conclusions unchanged: the
seven-task average on pair (c) is $77.05$ for our configuration against $74.39$
for TALAS ($+2.66$), on pair (b) $77.92$ against $75.82$ ($+2.10$), and on pair
(a) $79.25$ against $79.98$ ($-0.73$).
