\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

Let $f_T:\mathcal{X}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{d_T}$ be a frozen teacher and $f_S$ a
student with $L$ Transformer layers and width $d_S<d_T$. Writing
$\operatorname{norm}(\mathbf{x})=\mathbf{x}/(\lVert\mathbf{x}\rVert_2+\epsilon)$,
we run the teacher once over an unlabeled corpus $\{x_i\}_{i=1}^{N}$ and cache
only its final sentence embeddings,
$\widetilde{\boldsymbol{\tau}}_i=\operatorname{norm}(f_T(x_i))$. Pooling and
normalising the student's final token states gives
$\mathbf{z}_i\in\mathbb{S}^{d_S-1}$. The two vectors have different widths, so
nothing can be compared until a map bridges them; we fix that map before training
and never touch it again.

\subsection{Teacher Targets}
\label{sec:method-targets}

We fit the leading $d_S$-dimensional principal subspace
$P_{\mathrm{PCA}}\in\mathbb{R}^{d_T\times d_S}$ on the centred cache. Its
orientation inside that subspace is arbitrary --- invisible to every cosine-metric
evaluation, but not to a point-wise loss --- so we remove it once by orthogonal
Procrustes against the \emph{untrained} student. With $\mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{PCA}}$
the normalised principal coordinates of a fitting subset and $\mathbf{Z}_0$ the
student's normalised outputs on the same sentences
\citep{schonemann1966procrustes},
\begin{equation}
    R^\star=\underset{R\in O(d_S)}{\arg\min}\,
    \lVert\mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{PCA}}R-\mathbf{Z}_0\rVert_F=UV^\top,
    \qquad U\Sigma V^\top=\operatorname{svd}\!\left(\mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{PCA}}^\top\mathbf{Z}_0\right),
    \label{eq:gauge-alignment}
\end{equation}
and the target of sentence $x_i$ is
\begin{equation}
    \boldsymbol{\tau}_i
    =\operatorname{norm}\!\left(f_T(x_i)\,P_{\mathrm{PCA}}R^\star\right)
    \in\mathbb{S}^{d_S-1}.
    \label{eq:target}
\end{equation}
Both factors are fitted before the first gradient step and frozen for the whole
run. Because $R^\star$ is orthogonal it leaves the targets' pairwise cosines and
retained variance exactly unchanged, so the two factors are independent: the
projection decides \emph{what} of the teacher survives, the rotation only
\emph{where} it is placed relative to the student's starting point.

\subsection{Objective}
\label{sec:method-objective}

Only the final student embedding is supervised.  The endpoint term aligns each
student embedding with its fixed, dimension-matched teacher target:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{end}}
    =\frac{1}{B}\sum_{i=1}^{B}
    \left(1-\left\langle\mathbf{z}_i,\boldsymbol{\tau}_i\right\rangle\right),
    \label{eq:endpoint-loss}
\end{equation}
where $B$ is the minibatch size.  This term fixes point-wise correspondence in
the gauge selected by $P_{\mathrm{PCA}}R^\star$.

The second term matches the topology of the two embedding clouds without
requiring a shared ambient dimension.  Let
$\operatorname{Dgm}_k(\mathbf{X})$ denote the degree-$k$ Vietoris--Rips
persistence diagram of the row-wise normalised point cloud $\mathbf{X}$ under
the chord distance
$d(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})=\sqrt{2-2\langle\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}\rangle}$.
For $k=0$, the finite death times are exactly the $B-1$ edge weights of the
cloud's minimum spanning tree.  Writing their sorted values as
$\delta^{S}_{(j)}$ and $\delta^{T}_{(j)}$, we use
\begin{align}
    \mathcal{L}_{H_0}
    &=\frac{1}{B-1}\sum_{j=1}^{B-1}
      \left(\delta^{S}_{(j)}-\delta^{T}_{(j)}\right)^2, \\
    \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{topo}}
    &=\mathcal{L}_{H_0}
      +\lambda_{1}\,
      W_2^2\!\left(
        \operatorname{Dgm}_1(\mathbf{Z}),
        \operatorname{Dgm}_1(\widetilde{\mathbf{T}})
      \right),
    \label{eq:topological-loss}
\end{align}
where $W_2$ permits low-persistence cycles to match to the diagonal.  The
teacher cloud $\widetilde{\mathbf{T}}$ contains the original cached embeddings
$\widetilde{\boldsymbol{\tau}}_i\in\mathbb{R}^{d_T}$, not their projected
targets.  Consequently, $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{topo}}$ preserves connectivity
and cycles from the teacher's own space and is independent of the endpoint
interface.  Setting $\lambda_1=0$ gives the $H_0$-only variant.

The complete training objective contains only these two losses:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}
    =\lambda_{\mathrm{end}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{end}}
    +\lambda_{\mathrm{topo}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{topo}}.
    \label{eq:objective}
\end{equation}
The endpoint term transfers sample-wise semantics, while the topological term
constrains batch-level structure that a point-wise objective does not explicitly
measure.

\paragraph{What the method does not contain.}
No projector is trained, no contrastive or Gram loss is added, no intermediate
layer is supervised, and nothing is attached to the student at inference: the
deployed model is the unmodified encoder. Training costs one teacher pass over
the corpus, one truncated SVD of the cache, one $d_S\times d_S$ SVD for
$R^\star$, and then ordinary student training against the cached teacher
embeddings and their fixed projected targets --- no teacher hidden state,
attention map or online teacher forward pass is used.
