\begin{abstract}
Knowledge distillation is a practical route to compressing large text
embedding models into efficient encoders. Existing methods predominantly
formulate embedding transfer as \emph{state matching}: a student is trained to
reproduce a teacher embedding, intermediate representation, or relational
structure at one or more discrete depths. These objectives specify where a
student representation should be, but not how it should evolve through depth.
We introduce \textbf{GeoODE-KD}, a geometric dynamics formulation that instead
distills a teacher-guided representation trajectory. We place normalized
sentence embeddings on a hypersphere and define a semantic potential combining
instance-level alignment with the teacher's pairwise geometry. Its negative
Riemannian gradient induces an analytic vector field, while a finite-horizon
time reparameterization distributes the remaining semantic displacement across
student depth. Consecutive Transformer layers are treated as discrete
observations of this flow: an exponential-map update predicts the next
representation, and a dynamics-consistency objective trains the actual layer
transition to follow it. GeoODE-KD requires only cached final teacher
embeddings, introduces no learned flow module or numerical ODE solver, and
leaves the inference architecture unchanged. The ideal continuous dynamics
preserve embedding norms and monotonically decrease the teacher-conditioned
energy; in the instance-only case, their geodesic distance to the teacher
contracts in closed form and vanishes at the final depth.

\end{abstract}
