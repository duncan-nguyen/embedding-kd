\begin{abstract}
Knowledge distillation can compress text embedding models, but directly
matching teacher and student vectors is difficult when their dimensions and
coordinate systems differ. We propose GATE-KD, a final-embedding method that
first reduces teacher embeddings to the student dimension with a fixed PCA
projection and then fits their orientation to the student using closed-form
orthogonal Procrustes alignment. This produces pointwise training targets from
cached teacher embeddings without a trainable alignment module or intermediate
teacher states. As an auxiliary signal, GATE-KD also matches degree-zero
persistence summaries, namely distributions of connectivity scales, in the
original teacher and student spaces; this signal remains well-defined across
different dimensions. Under a label-free but task-adapted protocol spanning
three teacher--student configurations and nine sentence-level benchmarks,
GATE-KD achieves the highest numerical average among the evaluated distillation
methods, improving over the strongest baseline by 0.38--1.59 points. Controlled
experiments show that a per-minibatch rotation-invariant baseline trails
GATE-KD by 2.05 points; separate schedule controls associate most of this gap
with a corpus-level fitted gauge rather than repeated refitting, while $H_0$
adds 0.53 points over endpoint-only training.
\end{abstract}
