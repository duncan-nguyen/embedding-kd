\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

Early text-embedding distillation regressed student sentence vectors directly
onto teacher outputs, demonstrating dense supervision from unlabeled text
\citep{reimers2020multilingual}; later methods combine endpoint transfer with
contrastive, relative-geometric, multilingual, or specialized objectives
\citep{gao2021distilcse,xu2023distillcse,simtde2023,lealla2023,kim2023embeddistill,zhang2024jasper,li2025ese,leaf}
and with generated or task-targeted data \citep{lee2024gecko,akram2026jina}. A
separate family exploits the representation hierarchy: EMO aligns token-level
relations and hidden states, TALAS combines a teacher endpoint anchor with
lower-layer self-distillation, and others transfer mixtures of layers,
intermediate states, or attention
\citep{truong2025emo,talas,vu2026mol,wang2024feature}. Such supervision relies
on access to internal representations and on cross-model layer correspondence,
whose robustness has been questioned
\citep{ko2023revisiting,kim2025layerselection}. GATE-KD studies the
complementary terminal-only regime using cached final teacher embeddings.
Because evaluation settings differ across these systems, with EMO using
task-specific supervised training and TALAS a label-free task-adapted corpus,
we follow the TALAS data and evaluation protocol for a like-for-like comparison;
this controls corpus and task coverage but does not turn source-task results
into corpus-disjoint transfer.

Cross-dimensional endpoint supervision additionally requires a common output
space. Learned projection layers are widely used, but their parameterization
and normalization can materially alter student optimization
\citep{hpd,miles2024projector,chen2023projector}. TCS instead constructs reduced
coordinates from cached teacher features using PCA \citep{tcs}; the unaligned
row of Table~\ref{tab:gauge-mechanism} plays the role of this fixed-PCA target
in our matched comparison. After reduction, orthogonal changes of basis
preserve the retained inner products, so representation-similarity work treats
them as natural equivalences
\citep{kornblith2019cka,williams2021shape,maystre2025procrustes,han2026equivalence},
and \citet{bhattarai2025shortcut} show that unconstrained projected matching
can reach zero feature loss without preserving teacher Gram geometry, a
shortcut Procrustes avoids. Dimension consistency is also studied within a
single model: Matryoshka representation learning trains nested prefixes of one
embedding to remain useful at several truncation ranks \citep{kusupati2022mrl},
and MIPIC adds self-distillation between full and truncated embeddings with
intra-relational objectives \citep{huy2026mipic}. There the full embedding is
its own teacher; GATE-KD instead transfers a frozen external teacher of a
different width, where no shared parameterization links the two spaces and a
coordinate interface must be constructed. It combines a fixed PCA bottleneck
with a fitted, closed-form Procrustes gauge rather than a trainable projector;
the target-rank sensitivity in Appendix~\ref{app:target-rank} can be read as
treating the student width as a nested target rank of the teacher subspace.

Coordinate-free objectives offer a complementary route to transferring
structure. Relational, similarity-preserving, and contrastive distillation
match pairwise distances, Gram matrices, or positive--negative relations
\citep{park2019rkd,tung2019similarity,tian2020crd}; related methods impose
graph- or geometry-aware constraints in graph, dialogue, and diffusion settings
\citep{joshi2024representation,burykina2025jdcemb,zhou2026dice}. Persistent
homology extends this view to multiscale point-cloud structure: TopKD transfers
topology through approximate persistence images, topological-guidance
distillation uses persistence features from multiple teachers, and
topologically regularized embeddings impose prior topology on learned
low-dimensional representations
\citep{topkd2024,jeon2024topological,heiter2023topological}. GATE-KD uses
topology only as a dimension- and coordinate-invariant auxiliary, matching
sorted degree-zero persistence death times in the native teacher and student
spaces, while the explicit endpoint interface supplies the primary
correspondence signal.
